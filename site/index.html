packed-spotter

Packed and encrypted binary spotter in C++17, no dependencies. Sample: examples/upx-like.exe (synthetic, not real malware).

likely packed or encrypted, score 100/100

  • most of the file is near-random data (94% of windows at 7.2+ bits/byte)
  • section names used by known packers
  • a section with no data on disk but a large virtual size: the classic unpacking target
  • writable and executable section
  • high-entropy section
